Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

ShelfView ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that shows product information under search results on Amazon websites. This policy explains what the extension does with data.

  • ShelfView does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information.
  • ShelfView has no account, login, analytics or advertising.
  • Everything the extension does happens inside your own browser.

What the extension reads

When you open an Amazon search, Best Sellers, New Releases or Movers & Shakers page, ShelfView reads, from the same Amazon website you are visiting:

  • the product page of each product in the results (to find the Best Sellers Rank, brand and date first available), and
  • the product's "All offers" list (to find the Buy Box owner and the number of Amazon, FBA and FBM sellers).

It also reads the "bought in past month" text that Amazon shows on the search page, to estimate monthly sales.

These requests go directly from your browser to Amazon, the same way they would if you opened those pages yourself. ShelfView does not run on product, cart, checkout or sign-in pages.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, passwords, payment details or any other personal information.
  • We do not collect your browsing history or the products you look at.
  • We do not send any data to our own servers or to any third party.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade any data.
  • We do not use data for advertising, credit scoring or any purpose other than showing product information on the page.

Permissions

  • storage – to save your settings and the 12-hour product cache described above.
  • Access to Amazon websites – to show product information under search results and to read the product and offer pages listed above.

Children

ShelfView is a tool for Amazon sellers and is not directed at children.
